# Sonic Gallery privacy policy

Last updated 3 September 2026. This policy applies to Sonic Gallery 1.0.42 and later.

Sonic Gallery is a music player for the audio already on your Android device.
It has no account, no server and no analytics.

## What the app stores

The app keeps your playlists, favourites, settings (theme, equaliser,
crossfade, recent searches), where you left off in the last track, and a list
of the audio files found on your device. All of this stays in the app's
private storage on the device, which no other app can read. None of it is
sent anywhere. It is left out of cloud backup. A direct phone-to-phone
transfer carries it across.

## What the app does not do

It does not collect, share or sell personal data. It does not use analytics,
advertising or crash-reporting services. It makes no network requests of its
own.

## Permissions

Music and audio (`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`, or storage access on Android 12 and
below) lets the app list the audio on your device through Android's media
library. The app reads audio only, and only when you tap Scan Now or when the
app opens and you have already granted access.

Notifications are requested the first time music plays, so playback controls
can appear on the lock screen and in the notification shade.

Internet is used only when you ask for a lyrics search, which opens in your
browser, and in the browser preview to load sample artwork.

The foreground service (media playback) keeps music playing when the app is
in the background.

## Sharing

The Share button hands a line of text ("Listening to ... on Sonic Gallery") to
whichever app you choose. Sonic Gallery itself sends nothing.

## Children

The app is for a general audience. It collects no personal information from
anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Open Settings, then Storage & Data Safety, then tap Erase All User Data. This
removes everything the app stores. Uninstalling the app does the same.
